# AI-Powered Memecoin Radar Discord Bot

Python-based automated market intelligence system designed to analyze memecoins using real-time crypto market data, liquidity metrics, whale activity, holder concentration and token risk signals.

The system processes multiple market indicators, identifies positive signals and potential risks, and generates a structured BUY / AVOID assessment through Discord.

## Project Overview

Memecoin markets are highly volatile and often driven by liquidity changes, trading activity, whale movements and concentrated token ownership.

This project combines these indicators into a single automated analysis workflow.

The goal is not simple price prediction, but multi-factor risk and opportunity detection.

## Core Analysis Modules

### 1. Liquidity Analysis

- Token liquidity
- Liquidity changes
- Market depth
- Trading activity
- Liquidity risk detection

### 2. Volume & Market Activity

- Trading volume
- Volume changes
- Market momentum
- Abnormal activity detection
- Short-term market signals

### 3. Whale Activity

- Large transactions
- Whale movements
- Capital inflows and outflows
- Potential accumulation
- Potential distribution

### 4. Holder Concentration

The system evaluates token ownership concentration to identify potential risks associated with dominant wallets.

Key indicators include:

- Top-holder concentration
- Large wallet exposure
- Distribution risk
- Potential sell-pressure risk

### 5. Risk Analysis

Multiple indicators are combined to detect:

- Low liquidity
- Excessive holder concentration
- Abnormal whale activity
- Weak trading volume
- High volatility
- Potential market manipulation risk

## Signal Engine

The analysis engine combines positive and negative market signals into a structured assessment.

Example workflow:

Market Data
↓
Liquidity Analysis
↓
Volume Analysis
↓
Whale Activity
↓
Holder Concentration
↓
Risk Detection
↓
Signal Scoring
↓
BUY / AVOID Assessment

The final output is designed as a decision-support signal rather than an automatic trading instruction.

## Discord Integration

The project integrates the analysis engine with a Discord bot.

The bot can deliver:

- Token analysis
- Market metrics
- Positive signals
- Risk warnings
- Structured analysis results
- BUY / AVOID assessments

This allows market intelligence to be delivered directly through an automated Discord workflow.
